# Processing engines (Phase 2+)
Reserved integration points:
- video-analyzer: scene detection, frame sampling, Gemini video understanding
- segmentation: SAM 2 object/person masks and temporal propagation
- motion-tracking: keypoints, camera and object tracking
- character-rigging: 2D/3D rig preparation and motion retargeting
- compositing: occlusion, lighting, shadows and background repair
- rendering: FFmpeg-based final assembly and quality checks

Do not treat these folders as implemented AI engines yet.
